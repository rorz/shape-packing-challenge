found in these searches explains the current 2.9485269238856113 submission.
Do not invent the leader's algorithm from its score or submission name.

  the leader's team's earlier account reports large-neighborhood search with
  GJK polishing and a radius of 2.960586. This is an older, substantially worse
  packing, not evidence of the method behind today's score.

  much worse radii. Useful historical context; it does not resolve our decimal
  lattice problem.
- [Public Pack Semicircles Optimizer](https://github.com/compusophy/pack-semicircles):
  documents parallel tempering and local polishing in browser workers. No
  current leader-level score or six-decimal lattice method was established from
  the inspected README. Its performance/rigor claims are not independent proof.
